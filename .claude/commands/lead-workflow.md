# Complete Lead Generation Workflow

I'll guide you through a complete lead generation workflow that combines all the tools in this plugin.

## Workflow Overview

```
1. SEARCH → 2. ENRICH → 3. SCORE → 4. EXPORT
```

### Step 1: Search for Leads
First, I'll help you define search criteria to find potential leads in Apollo.io's database of 275M+ contacts.

### Step 2: Enrich Top Matches
I'll enrich the most promising leads (or all of them) to get complete contact information including emails and phone numbers.

### Step 3: Score Leads
I'll score each enriched lead based on data quality and relevance to your target criteria, assigning grades A-F.

### Step 4: Export Results
Finally, I'll export the scored leads to a CSV or JSON file, prioritized by score.

## Let's Get Started

Please provide your lead generation criteria:

**1. Target Persona:**
- Job titles you're targeting (e.g., "CTO", "VP of Engineering")
- Seniority levels (e.g., "c_suite", "vp", "director")
- Geographic locations (if applicable)

**2. Company Criteria:**
- Company size ranges (e.g., "51,200", "201,500" for mid-size companies)
- Industries or keywords (if applicable)

**3. Quantity:**
- How many leads do you want? (I recommend starting with 25-50)

**4. Export Preferences:**
- Format: CSV or JSON?
- Any specific fields you need?
